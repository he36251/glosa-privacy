## Common questions

**The model download is slow or stopped.**
The models are large (586 MB to 3.7 GB). Use Wi-Fi and keep enough free storage for the model you picked. If a download stops, tap the download button again.

**Does Glosa work offline?**
Yes. Once a model is downloaded, translation works in airplane mode. Ads and crash reports need a connection, and they don't affect translating.

**The camera doesn't read my language.**
Live camera translation reads Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari scripts. For other scripts, phones with 8 GB of memory can take a photo and let a Gemma model read it.

**Which phones are supported?**
iPhone with iOS 16 or later, and Android phones with a 64-bit ARM processor. Gemma 4 E4B works best with 8 GB of memory.

**How do I delete my data?**
Your translations, history and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling Glosa deletes all of them.

## Privacy

Glosa never uploads your translations, speech or photos. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy).
